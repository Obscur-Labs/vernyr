#### 1. Principles

**1.1 Purpose.** This Schedule explains our refund and cancellation rules. It follows Clauses 4.2 and 4.16 to 4.20 and Chapter 11 of the Rule Book.

**1.2 Post-service billing.** We do not collect our Service Fee in advance. We invoice only after a Service Milestone has been completed and delivered (Rule Book, Clause 4.2).

**1.3 No refund once invoiced and paid.** Because every invoice is for work already done, **the Service Fee, once invoiced and paid, is not refundable under any circumstances.**

**1.4 Work not done is not charged.** You never pay for a Service Milestone that had not started on the date you cancelled in writing.

**1.5 No hidden charges.** Your Fee Schedule shows all fees and taxes up front.

**1.6 Your legal rights.** Nothing in this Schedule removes your rights under the Consumer Protection Act 2019.

#### 2. Meanings in this Schedule

**2.1** "Cancellation" means your written notice ending the Engagement (Rule Book, Clause 11.4) or ending one or more Services.

**2.2** "Started" means that we have begun the work of a Service Milestone, for example: for Service A, the profile assessment for your shortlist has begun; for Service B, we have begun reviewing documents or drafting guidance for a specific application; for Service C, we have begun checking your visa documents or preparing your visa checklist.

#### 3. Cancellation by you

**3.1 Any time.** You may cancel at any time by written notice through the Portal or by email (Rule Book, Clause 11.4).

**3.2 What you pay.** You pay invoices for Service Milestones completed and delivered before your Cancellation. A Service Milestone that had Started before your Cancellation may be completed and invoiced. No Service Milestone that had not Started is invoiced.

**3.3 No refund of paid invoices.** Invoices already paid are not refunded (Clause 1.3).

**3.4 Post-dated cheques.** If you gave post-dated cheques under a separate payment agreement (Rule Book, Clause 4.15), cheques that relate only to Service Milestones that had not Started will be returned to you or cancelled and will not be presented.

#### 4. Amounts received for work never started

**4.1 Exceptional cases.** If, in an exceptional case, we have received any amount for a Service Milestone that was then never Started, that amount alone will, at your written choice, be refunded or adjusted against another Service Milestone (Rule Book, Clause 4.16(c)).

#### 5. Cases where no refund is ever given

**5.1 No refund cases.** In addition to Clause 1.3, the cases listed in Rule Book, Clause 4.17 never lead to a refund. In summary, these include: refusal of admission or scholarship; refusal or cancellation of a visa or permit, or refusal of entry; your change of mind after the work is done; your failure to give documents or attend appointments; false, forged or misleading documents or statements; hidden refusals or adverse history; failing a required test or an Offer condition; using another agent after engaging us; changes in law or policy; force majeure; termination by us under Rule Book, Clause 6.2 or Rule Book, Clause 11.5; and dissatisfaction with an outcome that is not caused by a failure of professional care and skill.

**5.2 Fake documents.** If the Engagement ends because of fake or altered documents or false statements, no refund of any kind is given, and all invoices for work done remain payable.

#### 6. Cancellation by us

**6.1 For a good reason with notice.** If we end the Engagement under Rule Book, Clause 11.6, we will not invoice any Service Milestone not yet completed, and any amount received for such a Service Milestone is refunded in full.

**6.2 For your breach.** If we end the Engagement under Rule Book, Clause 11.5, no refund is given and unpaid invoices become payable at once (Rule Book, Clause 11.7).

**6.3 Long force majeure.** If either side ends the Engagement after a force majeure event lasting more than 90 days (Rule Book, Clause 7.27), no further Service Milestone is invoiced, and paid invoices are not refunded.

#### 7. Third-Party Fees

**7.1 Their rules apply.** Application fees, tuition fees, deposits, visa fees, visa centre charges, biometric fees, insurance premiums, blocked-account charges, test fees, translation, attestation, courier and similar charges are governed only by the refund rules of the body you paid. We do not refund them (Rule Book, Clause 4.18).

**7.2 Our help with third-party refunds.** Some Institutions refund all or part of a tuition deposit if a visa is refused and proof is given. As a courtesy, we will tell you about the published refund rule and help you prepare the request. The decision and the timing belong to the Institution.

**7.3 Money paid through us.** If a Third-Party Fee was paid through us at your request and the third party refunds it to us, we pass on to you the amount we actually receive, less bank, exchange and remittance charges and taxes, within 30 days of receiving it.

#### 8. Changes, deferrals and transfers

**8.1 Changing plans.** A change of country, course, Institution or intake after work has Started is new work, quoted separately (Rule Book, Clause 4.10).

**8.2 Deferral before work starts.** If you defer to a later intake before a Service has Started, that Service is simply invoiced when it is done for the later intake, at the fee in your Fee Schedule, if the work is done within 12 months.

**8.3 No transfer to others.** Fees cannot be transferred to another person, including a sibling or friend.

#### 9. How to ask for a refund

**9.1 Written request.** Refunds arise only under Clauses 4, 6.1 and 7.3. Send your request through the Portal or by email to info.laeuropa@gmail.com. Include: your full name; the invoice or receipt number; the amount; the reason; and the bank account details of the person who made the payment.

**9.2 Timelines.** We will acknowledge your request within 2 Working Days, give you our written decision with reasons within 15 Working Days, and pay any approved refund within 30 days of our decision.

**9.3 How refunds are paid.** Refunds are paid to the original mode of payment or to the bank account of the person who made the payment. We do not pay refunds in cash or to any other person. We deduct only actual bank or payment gateway charges and any tax that the law does not allow us to recover. Where GST was charged, we issue a credit note and adjust GST as the law allows.

**9.4 Payment disputes with your bank.** Please contact us before raising a chargeback or payment dispute with your bank. If you raise one for work already done, we may give your bank our records of the work.

#### 10. If you disagree

**10.1 Complaints.** If you disagree with our decision, use the grievance process in Schedule 7. You may also approach a Consumer Commission under the Consumer Protection Act 2019.
