CONSENT TO SHARE MY APPLICATION WITH A UNIVERSITY
(Template prepared for La Europa. To be reviewed by La Europa's legal adviser before use.)

I agree that {company_legal_name} ("La Europa") may share my application with the university named on the screen, which is located outside India in the country shown.

What is shared: my name as in passport, gender, date of birth, nationality, passport number (masked unless the university needs it for offer or visa letters), city and state, academic records, English test results, work experience, and only the documents selected by my counsellor.
What is not shared: my phone number, personal email, home address, family and sponsor details, budget and finances, other universities I applied to, and La Europa's internal notes, unless I agree separately.
Why: only so the university can decide on my admission and issue offer, enrolment and visa letters.
Withdraw: I can withdraw this consent at any time in my student portal (Settings). La Europa will then remove the university's access to my documents. Data the university already received is then under the university's own responsibility.
