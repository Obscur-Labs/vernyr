DATA PROCESSING TERMS FOR PARTNER UNIVERSITIES
(Template prepared for La Europa. To be reviewed by La Europa's legal adviser before use.)

1. Purpose: you receive students' personal data from La Europa only to decide on their admission and to complete their enrolment (and, where needed, visa letters). You must not use it for any other purpose, including marketing.
2. Minimum data: La Europa shares only the fields and documents needed for admission. Do not ask students for data through other channels without La Europa.
3. Each student consented to the sharing of their application with your institution. If a student withdraws consent, La Europa will revoke access in the portal.
4. Security: keep downloaded files secure, limit access to staff who need it, and delete downloaded copies when no longer needed under your own retention rules. Watermarks and download logs are applied to every file.
5. Breaches: tell La Europa without undue delay (and in any case within 48 hours) if student data from the portal is lost, disclosed or accessed without authorisation.
6. You are responsible for meeting your own legal duties as a controller under the data protection laws that apply to you (for example the EU / UK GDPR).
7. Cross-border transfer: data is transferred from India to your country with the students' consent and in line with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023.
