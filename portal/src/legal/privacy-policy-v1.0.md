**1.1 Data Fiduciary and controller.** Euroverse Service LLP (trading as La Europa Education), 303, Panvel Point, Mota Varachha, Surat, Gujarat, India, is the Data Fiduciary under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 ("DPDP Act") and, where they apply, the data controller under the EU General Data Protection Regulation ("GDPR") and the UK GDPR, for the Personal Data described in this Schedule.

Where the law requires us to appoint a representative in the European Union or the United Kingdom, we will publish the representative's contact details on the Portal and our website.

**1.3 Who this Schedule covers.** This Schedule covers Personal Data of students, applicants, parents and guardians, Sponsors, and any other person whose data you give us for your file (for example a referee or a family member named in a document).

#### 2. Laws we follow

**2.1 India.** We follow the DPDP Act 2023 and the Digital Personal Data Protection Rules 2025, as they come into force in stages, and the Information Technology Act 2000.

**2.2 Europe and the United Kingdom.** Where we offer our Services to people in the European Union or the United Kingdom, we also follow the GDPR, the UK GDPR and the UK Data Protection Act 2018.

**2.3 Other countries.** Where the data protection law of another country applies to our processing of your data, we follow it to the extent it applies.

**2.4 Highest standard.** Where more than one law applies, we try to follow the stricter standard.

#### 3. What Personal Data we collect

**3.1 Categories.** Depending on the Services you take, we collect:

(a) **identity data:** name, date of birth, gender, nationality, photograph, signature;

(b) **contact data:** address, email, phone and WhatsApp numbers;

(c) **passport and travel data:** passport details and copies, previous visas, travel history;

(d) **academic data:** certificates, mark sheets, transcripts, degrees, backlogs, gaps, test scores;

(e) **professional data:** CV, experience letters, salary slips where required;

(f) **family and Sponsor data:** names, relationship, contact details, occupation and income documents of parents, guardians and Sponsors;

(g) **financial data:** bank statements, loan letters, income tax returns, property or asset documents, blocked-account confirmations, and payment records with us;

(h) **immigration history:** previous applications, refusals, bans and overstays;

(i) **health data:** only where an Institution or Authority requires it, such as a medical certificate, a test report or a disability-related adjustment request;

(j) **police clearance or criminal record certificates:** only where an Institution or Authority requires them;

(k) **content you create:** Statement of Purpose, motivation letters, essays and messages;

(l) **communication records:** Portal messages, emails, WhatsApp messages, call notes and call recordings (Rule Book, Clause 9.9);

(m) **Portal technical data:** login times, IP address, device and browser type, pages viewed within the Portal, reading time of legal documents, and security logs; and

(n) **consent records:** what you agreed to, when, and which version.

**3.2 We collect only what is needed.** We collect only data needed for the Services or required by an Institution, Authority or law. We do not ask for data we do not need.

**3.3 Biometrics.** We do not collect your fingerprints or facial biometrics. Authorities and visa centres collect these directly from you under their own laws.

#### 4. Where we get your data

**4.1 Sources.** We get your data: from you; from your parent, guardian or Sponsor; from Institutions and Application Platforms (for example decisions and Offers); from Authorities and visa partners (for example appointment confirmations); from a Sub-agent who introduced you; from a referred provider you chose (for example a loan sanction letter you asked them to send us); and from the Portal itself (technical data).

**4.2 Data about other people.** If you give us data about another person (for example a parent or Sponsor), you confirm that you have told that person and that they agree. Their data is protected in the same way as yours.

#### 5. Why we use your data and our legal basis

**5.1 Purposes.** We use your data for these purposes:

(a) to provide the Services under the Rule Book and your Fee Schedule;

(b) to prepare and submit applications to Institutions and Application Platforms;

(c) to prepare visa and residence permit files and book appointments;

(d) to communicate with you about your file (Schedule 5);

(e) to issue invoices and receipts, collect fees and keep tax and accounting records;

(f) to keep the Portal secure, prevent fraud and detect fake documents;

(g) to keep a record of your consent and of the documents you accepted;

(h) to handle complaints and legal claims;

(i) to follow the law, court orders and lawful requests from authorities;

(j) to train our staff and improve our service quality, using records in a way that protects your privacy;

(k) to send marketing messages, only if you give separate consent (Schedule 5); and

(l) to publish testimonials or results, only if you give separate written consent (Rule Book, Clause 9.12).

**5.2 Legal basis.** Under the DPDP Act, we process your data on the basis of your consent, or for a legitimate use allowed by section 7 of the DPDP Act (for example where you have voluntarily given data for a specific purpose, or to comply with law or a court order). Where the GDPR or UK GDPR applies, our legal bases are: performance of our contract with you (purposes (a) to (e)); our legal obligations (purposes (e) and (i)); our legitimate interests in security, fraud prevention, record-keeping, defending claims and service quality (purposes (f) to (h) and (j)); and your consent (purposes (k) and (l), and any processing of health data, where consent is the basis).

**5.3 New purposes.** We will not use your data for a new purpose without telling you first and, where required, asking for your consent.

#### 6. Notice and consent

**6.1 Clear notice.** This Schedule, together with the Student Declaration & Consent, is our notice to you. It tells you what data we collect, why, and how you can use your rights, as required by the DPDP Act and Rules.

**6.2 Separate consents.** In the Student Declaration & Consent, your consent to data processing, to data sharing for applications and visas, and to transfers outside India are each given as separate, specific statements. **6.4 Consent Managers.** When registered Consent Managers become available under the DPDP Rules, you may also give, manage or withdraw your consent through a Consent Manager, and we will accept it.

#### 7. Who we share your data with

**7.1 Universities and visa.** Sharing with Institutions, Application Platforms, Authorities and visa partners is explained in Schedule 4.

**7.2 Our service providers.** We use trusted service providers who process data for us, under contracts that require confidentiality and security. These include: cloud hosting and the Portal software platform; email and messaging services; payment gateways and banks; accounting and tax advisers; electronic signature providers; and IT support providers. They may use your data only to provide their service to us.

**7.3 Sub-agents.** If you came to us through a Sub-agent who manages your file, that Sub-agent uploads your documents and can see your file, documents and messages on the Portal, as needed to handle your applications. The Sub-agent must protect your data under the La Europa Education Agent Constitution. A referral partner who only introduced you and does not manage your file sees only basic status information.

**7.4 Referred providers.** We share your details with a referred provider (Rule Book, Clause 3.10) only after you ask for the referral and agree.

**7.5 Legal disclosures.** We may disclose data where required by law, a court order or a lawful request from a government authority, or to protect our legal rights.

**7.6 Business transfer.** If our business is transferred to a successor (Rule Book, Clause 14.6), your data may be transferred to it, and it must protect your data under this Schedule.

**7.7 Never sold.** We never sell, rent or trade your Personal Data.

#### 8. Transfers outside India

**8.1 Why transfers happen.** Applying abroad means your data must be sent to Institutions, Application Platforms and Authorities in other countries. Some of our service providers may also store data outside India.

**8.2 Indian law.** We transfer data outside India only as allowed under section 16 of the DPDP Act, and not to any country the Government of India has restricted.

**8.3 European and UK law.** Where the GDPR or UK GDPR applies, transfers from the EU or UK to India and other countries are made on a lawful basis, such as standard contractual clauses where available, or where the transfer is necessary for our contract with you or made at your request for your application.

#### 9. How long we keep your data

**9.1 Standard period.** We keep your Personal Data for 7 (seven) years after the Engagement ends. We keep it this long because tax, GST and accounting laws require us to keep records for long periods, and because we may need records to answer complaints or defend legal claims.

**9.2 Longer or shorter.** We keep data longer if the law requires it or a dispute or investigation is pending. Data you never submitted for any purpose (for example an abandoned draft) may be deleted sooner.

**9.3 Logs.** We keep Portal security logs for at least one year, or longer where the law requires.

**9.4 After the period.** After the retention period, we delete your data or make it anonymous so that it can no longer identify you.

**9.5 Inactive users.** Where the law requires us to erase data of a person who has not used our Services for a set period, we will do so, after informing you in advance as the law requires.

#### 10. How we protect your data

**10.1 Safeguards.** We use reasonable security safeguards suited to the data we hold, including: encrypted connections to the Portal; encryption or masking of stored data where practical; access only for staff who need it for your file; strong passwords and two-step login for staff; activity logs; regular backups; confidentiality undertakings and training for staff; checks on our service providers; and a plan for responding to incidents.

**10.2 Your part.** Keep your password secret, log out on shared devices, and tell us at once about any suspicious activity.

#### 11. Data breaches

**11.1 What we do.** If a breach of Personal Data happens, we will act at once to contain it, inform affected persons without delay (explaining what happened, the likely effects, what we are doing, what you can do, and whom to contact), and report it to the Data Protection Board of India, including a detailed report within 72 hours where the DPDP Rules require. Where the GDPR or UK GDPR applies, we will also notify the relevant supervisory authority within the time those laws require.

#### 12. Your rights

**12.1 Rights under Indian law.** Under the DPDP Act you have the right to:

(a) get a summary of your Personal Data we process, the processing activities, and the identities of others we have shared it with;

(b) have your data corrected, completed and updated;

(c) have your data erased, unless we must keep it by law or for a pending purpose;

(d) withdraw your consent;

(e) have your grievance addressed by us (Schedule 7); and

(f) nominate another person to use your rights if you die or become unable to act.

**12.2 Additional rights under European and UK law.** Where the GDPR or UK GDPR applies, you also have the rights to restrict processing, to object to processing based on legitimate interests, to data portability, and not to be subject to a decision based solely on automated processing that significantly affects you.

**12.3 How to use your rights.** Make your request through the Portal or by email to info.laeuropa@gmail.com. We may ask you to confirm your identity. We will reply within 30 days, or within any shorter time set by law. If we need more time for a complex request, we will tell you why, as the law allows. We do not charge for reasonable requests.

**12.4 If you are not satisfied.** You must first use our grievance process (Schedule 7). You may then complain to the Data Protection Board of India, or, where the GDPR or UK GDPR applies, to your data protection authority.

**12.5 Your duties.** Under the DPDP Act, you must give true information, must not impersonate anyone, must not hide material information, and must not make false or frivolous complaints.

#### 13. Children

**13.1 Rules for under-18s.** We process data of a person under 18 only with the verifiable consent of a parent or lawful guardian. We do not track, monitor the behaviour of, or send targeted advertising to anyone under 18. A person under 13 cannot register. Details are in Schedule 8.

#### 14. Software, automation and AI tools

**14.1 No automated decisions.** We do not make any decision about you that has legal or similarly significant effects solely by automated means. Admission and visa decisions are made by Institutions and Authorities, not by us and not by our software.

**14.2 Tools we use.** We may use software tools, including tools with artificial intelligence features, to organise files, check documents for completeness, draft messages and translate. We choose providers who are bound by confidentiality and data protection terms, and a member of our team reviews the output before it is used in your file.
